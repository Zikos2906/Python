Services

Services is a central point in Zoho People where all the HR processes can be accessed. It is a digital HR service provided to an employee. Under Services, all system services such as Leave, Attendance, and Timesheet will be available. Additionally, admins can create custom services for the use of the employees in their organization. The person who creates a custom service automatically becomes the Service Owner. The Service Owner can be changed while editing the service.
